Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy covers the apps made by T Panda Atelier, a solo developer: Bambowl, Quillix Note and Cypher Vault (“the apps”). In short: the apps keep your data on your device, sync it only if you sign in, never show ads, never track you, and never sell anything about you.

What the apps collect

Without signing in

Everything you create (recipes, meal plans, notes, vault entries, settings) is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

When you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app. We use them only to identify your account and show who is signed in. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

While you are signed in, the content you create in an app is copied to a secure database so it can sync between your devices. Each account can read and change only its own data. Cypher Vault encrypts your vault on your device before it is uploaded, so the stored copy cannot be read without your master password.

Bambowl specifics

  • Recipe from a link: when you import a link, Bambowl fetches that page (for example a recipe site, YouTube or TikTok) to read the recipe. This happens only when you ask.
  • Facebook, Instagram and TikTok sign-ins: if you sign in to these inside Bambowl's built-in browser, those sign-ins stay on your phone. We never receive your passwords or those accounts' data.
  • AI tidy-up: when you tap Tidy up, the recipe text you chose is sent to an AI model through OpenRouter, using your own OpenRouter key if you added one or the app's. Only that text is sent, and only when you tap.
  • New accounts: when someone signs in to Bambowl for the first time, the developer receives an email with that person's name and email address.

Services we use

  • Google: sign-in.
  • Supabase: account sign-in and the database that stores synced data (hosted in Tokyo, Japan).
  • OpenRouter: AI tidy-up in Bambowl, only for the text you choose.
  • Resend: sends the developer the new-account email.

These services process data only to provide these functions. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Google user data

The apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google account information is used only to sign you in and sync your own data. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone except the services listed above that are needed to run the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

Synced data is kept for as long as you keep your account. Signing out stops syncing; the data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. To delete your account and all synced data, email dekloongjohn@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with. It will be deleted within 30 days. You can also remove the apps' access at any time in your Google Account under Security › Third-party apps and services.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
