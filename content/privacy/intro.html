In short: the apps keep your data on your device, sync it only if you sign in, never show ads, never track you, and never sell anything about you.

The basics, for every app

Without signing in

Everything you create (recipes, notes, vault entries, settings) is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

When you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app. We use them only to identify your account and show who is signed in. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

While you are signed in, the content you create in an app is copied to a secure database (Supabase) so it can sync between your devices. Each account can read and change only its own data.

Update checks

Apps downloaded from Zenith Gen Orbit check once a day for a newer version by downloading a small file from this website. Nothing about you is sent.
