The basics

Without signing in

Everything you create{{contentParen}} is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

When you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with {{apps}}. We use them only to identify your account and show who is signed in. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

While you are signed in, what you create is copied to a secure database (Supabase) so it can sync between your devices. Each account can read and change only its own data.
