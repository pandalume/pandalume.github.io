Services we use

  • Google: sign-in.
  • Supabase: account sign-in, the database that stores synced data and Bambowl's synced photos (hosted in Singapore for Bambowl, and in Tokyo, Japan for the other apps).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers Bambowl's alerts for friends' messages and requests.
  • OpenRouter: AI tidy-up in Bambowl, only for the text you choose.
  • Resend: sends the developer the new-account email.
  • GitHub: hosts this website and the app downloads. GitHub counts how many times each file is downloaded; we see only those totals.

These services process data only to provide these functions. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Google user data

The apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google account information is used only to sign you in and sync your own data. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone except the services listed above that are needed to run the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

Synced data is kept for as long as you keep your account. Signing out stops syncing; the data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. To delete your account and all synced data, email {{contact}} from the email address you signed in with. It will be deleted within 30 days. You can also remove the apps' access at any time in your Google Account under Security › Third-party apps and services.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
