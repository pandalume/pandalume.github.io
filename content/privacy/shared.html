This website and downloads

Zenith Gen Orbit and its downloads are hosted by GitHub. GitHub counts how many times each file is downloaded; we see only those totals. This site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other websites.

The services listed for each app process data only to provide those functions. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Google user data

The apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google account information is used only to sign you in and sync your own data. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone except the services listed for each app that are needed to run it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Synced data is kept for as long as you keep your account. Signing out stops syncing; the data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. To delete your account and all synced data, email {{contact}} from the email address you signed in with and say which app. It will be deleted within 30 days. You can also remove an app's access at any time in your Google Account under Security › Third-party apps and services.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a change affects data you have already given us, we will say so in the app.
