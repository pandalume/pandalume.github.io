This website and downloads

Zenith Gen Orbit and its downloads are hosted by GitHub. GitHub counts how many times each file is downloaded; we see only those totals. This site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other websites. One exception: in the installed Zenith app on Android, tapping an Android download asks our Supabase server for the file's direct download address, so the download starts properly. Only the file's address is sent, nothing about you.

The Family page is for people we have invited. Signing in there uses your Google account through our Supabase server (Singapore), which checks your email address against the invite list. Your sign-in is kept in your browser until you sign out. The invite list holds only the email addresses and names we add, and which apps each person may see.

The services listed above process data only to provide those functions. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.
