Bambowl

  • My fridge: the list of what you have at home (Groceries › My fridge) is kept on your phone and, while you are signed in, synced with your preferences so your other phones have it. Working out what a recipe is missing happens on your phone.
  • Photos: while you are signed in, a small copy of each recipe's main photo is stored with your synced data, readable only by your account. Gallery photos you add to a recipe stay on your phone.
  • Friends: if you use Friends, people you add can see your name, profile picture, profile level, badges, the frame and chat style you wear, and how many recipes you have, never the recipes themselves. Others can find you only by your exact email address or your Bambowl ID, and only after you accept their request do they become friends. Nobody can browse a list of users, except Bambowl's owner, who can look people up by name or Bambowl ID (seeing only the name, Bambowl ID and profile picture) to send them a message or a gift from Bambowl.
  • Messages: chat messages and recipes you send a friend are stored so both of you can read them, and only you two can read them. A shared recipe photo is deleted once your friend saves the recipe, or after 30 days at most. You can remove or block a friend at any time.
  • Groups: if you join a group (up to 5 people, invited by someone in it who is your friend), everyone in the group can see your name, profile picture, profile level, badges and the frame and chat style you wear, the messages you send to the group and the recipes you share in it, never your other recipes. They are stored so the group can read them, and only people in the group can. A recipe photo shared in a group is kept while the recipe is shared there and deleted within a day or two after it is removed or the group ends. When the last person leaves a group, or its owner ends it, its messages are deleted. You can mute a group, leave it, or block someone at any time.
  • Reporting a person: if you report someone, we store and email to the developer: both people's email addresses and Bambowl names and IDs, the reason, any note you add (up to 500 characters), and the last 20 messages between you (for someone in a group: the group's name and each person's last 20 messages in it). This is used only to deal with the report and prevent abuse. A report is kept even if either account is later deleted, but it is no longer linked to the account.
  • Keeping chats kind: messages, group names, and profile names and about lines are checked on Bambowl's server against a list of explicit words, which are replaced with stars. Someone reported by three or more different people within 30 days can't create or join groups until the reports have been looked at.
  • Bambits, quests, achievements and Cosmetics: while you are signed in, Bambowl's server keeps your Bambits balance and a history of each change (what it was for and when), your daily gift streak and your phone's time zone (so your day starts at your midnight), your daily and weekly quests and achievement progress, which it works out from your synced recipes, plans, cookbooks, shopping list, friends and shared recipes, the frames, chat styles and collectibles you have and wear, what you buy in the Shop with Bambits (and when), the events you finish, the codes you redeem and the season pass rewards you claim. When you mark a recipe cooked, the date and your phone's hour are saved with it (one achievement is for cooking after midnight). Only you can see your balance, history, quests and progress; friends and group members see your badges and what you wear. Bambits are earned only and can't be bought with money or cashed out; the Shop takes only Bambits.
  • Mail: messages Bambowl sends you (a gift, the reward for a redeem code, an event or news) are kept on Bambowl's server with when you read them and claim what's attached. Only you can read your Mail. You can delete a message once anything attached has been claimed.
  • Alerts: to tell you about new messages, group messages, group invites and friend requests while Bambowl is closed, your phone's notification address (a token from Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging) is stored with your account. The alert shows the sender's name and the start of the message. Signing out removes it.
  • Recipe from a link: when you import a link, Bambowl fetches that page (for example a recipe site, YouTube, TikTok or Xiaohongshu) to read the recipe. This happens only when you ask.
  • Nutrition: Bambowl shows nutrition only as a recipe site publishes it, and never estimates it. For a saved recipe that came from a recipe site and has no nutrition yet, Bambowl reads that site's page once more in the background, once per recipe, just to copy the published numbers. The site sees an ordinary page request that names Bambowl. Nothing about you or your recipes is sent. Social media and video links are skipped.
  • Fresh picks: Discover's daily picks are chosen by Bambowl's server from public recipe sites, and are the same for everyone. Their photos load straight from those sites, which see an ordinary visit. When you save a pick, Bambowl keeps a copy of the recipe and its photo.
  • Facebook, Instagram and TikTok sign-ins: if you sign in to these inside Bambowl's built-in browser, those sign-ins stay on your phone. We never receive your passwords or those accounts' data.
  • Photo to text: photos of recipes are read into text on your phone. They are not uploaded for this.
  • Hands-free cooking: if you switch on hands-free in cooking mode, Bambowl uses your phone's microphone, only while cooking mode is open and Bambowl is on screen, to hear commands such as “next”, and reads steps aloud with your phone's text-to-speech. Your speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech service, on the phone where it supports that; otherwise that service (usually Google) may process it. If you switch on “Hear me from further away” in Preferences, your speech is sent to Google's online speech service to be turned into text, only while hands-free is on. The voice that reads steps aloud is one of the voices on your phone. When the recipe is in Chinese, the same services listen and read in Chinese. Bambowl does not record or keep what you say, and sends none of it to Bambowl's server. It is off until you switch it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Updates: when you tap Update, Bambowl downloads the new version from GitHub, where Zenith Gen Orbit keeps its files, checks it, and hands it to Android, which asks you to confirm. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Deleting your account: in Bambowl, go to Account › Google account › Delete my account. This deletes at once your synced data, synced and shared photos, profile, friends, chats, the messages and recipes you shared in groups, your Bambits, achievements, Cosmetics and Mail, your place in each group (if you made a group, the person who has been in it longest takes it over), alert registrations and your sign-in. Recipes stored on your phone stay until you erase them or uninstall the app. You can also email us to delete it (see below).
  • Backups: a copy of Bambowl's server database is saved each day on the developer's own computer and kept for 14 days, then deleted. So deleted data can remain in a backup for up to 14 days.

Services Bambowl uses

  • Google: sign-in.
  • Supabase (servers in Singapore): accounts, synced data, synced main photos, friends, messages, groups, reports, Bambits, achievements, Cosmetics and Mail.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers alerts for friends' messages and requests, and for groups.
  • Your phone's speech and text-to-speech services (for example Google's): hands-free cooking, only while you have it switched on.
  • GitHub: hosts the update files.
  • Recipe websites: the pages you import, Fresh picks photos, and published nutrition for recipes saved from them.
  • Resend: sends the developer the report emails.
