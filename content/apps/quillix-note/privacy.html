Quillix Note

  • Notes and handwriting, with the pictures, recordings and files in them, are stored on your device. Nothing leaves your device until you sign in.
  • Sync (optional): when you sign in with Google, your email address and your notes are stored with your account so they sync between your devices. The contents of locked notes are encrypted on your device with your password before they are uploaded.
  • Sharing (optional): when you share a note, the note and the email addresses of the people you share it with are stored so they can open it. Stopping sharing removes it.
  • AI features (optional): AI is off until you add your own key in AI settings. You choose the service: Anthropic (Claude), or any OpenAI-compatible server, including one running on your own computer. The same applies to an optional transcription server. When you tap an AI or transcription action, the text, handwriting image or recording involved is sent to that service under your own key and its terms. Nothing is sent otherwise, and nothing goes to us. Your keys are stored on your device only.
  • Updates: once a day the app reads a small file from our download site to see whether a new version is out. When you tap Update, the new version downloads inside the app from the same place and is checked before it installs. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Deleting your data: Settings › Cloud sync › Delete my cloud data removes your synced notes, files and shared notes from our server. To delete your sign-in account as well, email us. Uninstalling the app removes the notes from that device.

Services Quillix Note uses

  • Google: sign-in.
  • Supabase (servers in Singapore): accounts, synced notes and shared notes.
  • GitHub, which hosts Zenith Gen Orbit: update information and downloads.
  • The AI or transcription service you choose, only if you turn it on with your own key.
