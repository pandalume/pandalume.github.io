Quillix Note

  • Notes and handwriting are stored on your device. While you are signed in they are copied to your account so they sync between your devices.
  • AI features (optional): AI is off until you add your own key in AI settings. You choose the service: Anthropic (Claude), or any OpenAI-compatible server, including one running on your own computer. When you tap an AI action, the text, handwriting image or recording involved is sent to that service under your own key and its terms. Nothing is sent otherwise, and nothing goes to us. Your keys are stored on your device only.

Services Quillix Note uses

  • Google: sign-in.
  • Supabase (servers in Singapore): accounts and synced notes.
  • The AI service you choose, only if you turn AI on with your own key.
